Which list should I choose?

Continuing Education (labeled “Continuing Education – …”) lists short, non-credit classes for workforce or personal enrichment.

Curriculum terms (e.g., “Fall 2025”) list for-credit courses that count toward degrees/certificates.

You can switch anytime—search & filters apply to whichever list you select.

Delivery methods explained

Online: Coursework happens online. Some classes may still require proctored tests or specific due dates.

Hybrid: A mix of online work and in-person meetings on set days/times.

Traditional: Fully in-person, meeting on campus at scheduled days/times.

Tip: The language icon indicates an online component.
